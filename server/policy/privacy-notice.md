Captain H. Francisco Elementary School – Main uses the Parent's Portal System to share each learner's grades, attendance and school news with their parents. This notice explains what personal information the portal handles, why, who can see it, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

• Account details of every user: full name, Login ID (a parent's Login ID is their child's LRN; staff use the ID the school assigns), password, role, the account's status, who created or approved the account, and the email address or mobile number (either or both) you give and confirm for codes and alerts. A profile photo is optional.

• Learner records: the learner's name, LRN, grade level and section, status, scores and grades, attendance, excuse letters (with their reasons and the adviser's remarks), and the parent's acknowledgement of each term's grades.

• Messages and posts: messages between parents and teachers, between teachers, and between teachers and the principal, plus the announcements, memos and events the school posts.

• Security records: failed sign-in attempts with the network (IP) address they came from, the network (IP) address of each request for a code, a log of account activity, and when you accepted this notice and the Terms of Use.

We do not collect learners' birthdays, gender, home addresses or contact numbers.

Why we use it

• To let parents follow their child's grades, attendance and school announcements.

• To let teachers record grades and attendance and talk with parents.

• To let the principal manage accounts and lock final grades.

• To keep accounts secure and help users get back into their accounts.

We use your information only for these school purposes. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

Consent for learners

Learners are minors and do not have their own accounts. When a parent first signs in, they give consent on their child's behalf.

Who can see it

• Parents see only their own child's records and their own messages.

• Teachers see the learners of the classes they teach and the sections they advise. Among teachers, only a learner's adviser records attendance and reviews excuse letters.

• The principal, as the portal's administrator, can see all records and manages accounts, learner records, advisers, grade locks, settings, backups and the activity log.

• The student developers from Pateros Technological College may access the data only to maintain the portal and fix problems during the capstone project and the pilot.

Where it is stored

• The portal's database is stored with Supabase, a cloud database service, in its Singapore data center.

• The portal's server runs on a computer or an online hosting service maintained by the student developers. Backup copies of the database are kept there too.

• Codes and alerts by email are sent through Google's Gmail, and those by text message through a text-messaging service.

How we protect it

• Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.

• Repeated wrong sign-ins are blocked for a while.

• Verification codes expire after 10 minutes.

• Each user sees only what their role allows.

• An alert goes to every email address and mobile number you have confirmed whenever your password changes.

How long we keep it

We keep the information while the learner is enrolled and the account is active. When the capstone project and the pilot end, the data is deleted or handed over to the school, as the principal decides.

On your device

The portal keeps your sign-in, your display settings and any unsent form text on your own device, so you stay signed in and don't lose what you typed.

Your rights

Under RA 10173, you have the right to:

• be informed of how your information is used;

• see the information the portal holds about you or your child;

• ask for wrong information to be corrected;

• object to its use, or withdraw your consent;

• ask for it to be deleted or blocked;

• get a copy of it;

• claim damages as the law allows, and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Withdrawing consent closes the account, because the portal cannot work without it.

Contact

For questions or requests about your information, contact the school office at (02) 8350-6114.

Changes to this notice

When this notice changes, the portal shows the new version and asks you to accept it before you continue.
