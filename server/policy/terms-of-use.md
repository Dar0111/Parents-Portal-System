By using the Parent's Portal System of Captain H. Francisco Elementary School – Main, you agree to these terms.

Accounts

• Only the school creates accounts. The principal creates teacher accounts. Each adviser creates the parent accounts for their own section, and the principal approves them.

• Each learner has one parent account. A parent signs in with the learner's LRN as the Login ID.

• At first sign-in, you confirm an email address (such as Gmail) or a mobile number you control and set your own password.

• Keep your password private and do not share your account. You are responsible for what is done with it.

• If you think someone else used your account, change your password or use "Forgot password", and tell the adviser or the school.

Acceptable use

• Use the portal only for school matters.

• Be respectful in messages and excuse letters.

• Do not post false or offensive content, or other people's private information.

• Do not try to open records that are not yours, or to disrupt the portal.

Records

• Grades, attendance and announcements come from the school. The report card (SF9) remains the school's official record.

• If a grade or an attendance entry looks wrong, tell the learner's adviser.

• Sending an excuse letter does not approve it; the adviser reviews it. If the adviser approves it, an absent or unrecorded class day is marked Excused; if declined, an unrecorded class day is marked Absent.

Availability

The portal is a capstone project of students of Pateros Technological College, tested with the school in a pilot. It may be unavailable during maintenance or when the internet is down, and its features may change during the pilot.

Closing accounts

The school may deactivate an account that is misused or no longer needed, such as when the learner leaves the school.

Privacy

The Data Privacy Notice explains how the portal handles personal information.

Changes to these terms

When these terms change, the portal shows the new version and asks you to accept it before you continue.

Contact

For questions, contact the learner's adviser or the school office at (02) 8350-6114.
